# Project summary

SoilQuest AI compares four resource-constrained environmental sampling strategies on entirely synthetic spatial fields. A trained Gaussian-process model updates from acquired observations only; the app visualizes uncertainty, shortest obstacle-aware paths, a rotating 3D concentration surface and complete decision audits.

Truthful resume bullet: Directed an AI-assisted prototype of an environmental sampling laboratory; evaluated four sampling policies over 96 synthetic episodes with Gaussian-process reconstruction and explicit sample/travel constraints.

Contribution disclosure: Moosa supplied the project direction and requirements and may review or modify the results. Codex generated the implementation, tests, analysis graphics and documentation. No field research, actual heavy-metal detection or real-world performance is established by this work.
